About

I am a physicist specializing in astrophysics instrumentation, detector systematics, and their impact on precision cosmology. My current focus is commissioning and science validation for Instrument Signature Removal (ISR) in the Rubin Observatory Data Management Science Pipeline, alongside forward modeling of image systematics for the Dark Energy Science Collaboration.

My graduate work at UC Irvine and SLAC spanned LSSTCam operations, electro-optical sensor systematics, and deep-learning approaches to Galactic gamma-ray emission for dark matter searches with Fermi-LAT. Earlier research at UC Davis and LBNL included liquid xenon dark matter experiments and energy systems modeling.
